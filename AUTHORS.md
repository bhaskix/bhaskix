## Attribution policy

- Every contributor is credited here, regardless of the size of the contribution. Documentation,
  design review, testing, and translation count.
- Commits carry `Signed-off-by` (DCO) — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Copyright is retained by each contributor. Bhaskix does not require a copyright assignment.
- Security reporters are credited in the advisory unless they prefer otherwise
  ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md) §9).
